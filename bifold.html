Bifold garage doors that clear the whole opening

Panels fold flat to one side or both, on a track at the head and a guide at the floor, nothing across the ceiling. Automated with an operator we fit in the shop, or pushed by hand. Up to 40 ft wide, with a man door in the run if you want one.

Why bifold. The garage becomes a room with a wall that goes away.

An overhead door takes the ceiling; a bifold leaves it alone, so the garage works as a studio, a gym, a car-lift bay or the room that opens onto the yard. Closed, the inside face matches the outside, with the same wood and the same finish.

Each leaf is a stiff, flat panel hung from the head track, so a 10-foot-tall door swings as easily as a cabinet door. We build the operator mount, the arm and the brackets, and tune the whole thing before it leaves.

Any face we make goes on a bifold: flush plank, slats, painted rail-and-stile with a lite band, glass. The fold pattern is yours too: three left and one right, two and two, all to one side.
