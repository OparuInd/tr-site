Why a set. The garage door is rarely alone on the wall.

A stainless inlay that runs across the garage door should continue, at the same height, across the gate beside it. A pivot entry door should be the same plank as the garage. We build them together, from the same boards, in the same week.

Driveway gates swing or slide, automated. Pedestrian gates take a keypad or a latch. Entry doors hinge or pivot, with glass or without.
